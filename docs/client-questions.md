# Fishbone Graphics, questions for Jack

A running list. Add to it as they come up. When it is ready it becomes the
brief we hand the shop, styled, with the site next to it. Group headings
are the sections of that brief.

Updated 2026-09-07.

## Inventory and stock

1. Do you manage inventory in any software today, or is it a shelf and a
   notebook? Would you want to run inventory in your own system, inside
   Pulse, or keep doing what you do?
2. Do you count blanks in dozens, in pieces, or both? We can make the
   system take either and show both.
3. Is there printed stock on the shelf today? What, how many, which sizes?
4. Do you keep misprints, seconds or overruns? Would you sell them online at
   a discount if the sizes are random? On the site this is "the randoms",
   off the pile, priced to sell, no returns. Tyler is leaning no returns.
   It can carry old event shirts as vintage. Confirm the no returns line.
5. When someone takes blanks off the shelf, do you want that logged with
   initials and a time, so the count stays honest?

## Blanks and the catalog

6. What brands, style numbers and colors do you actually buy? We can match
   everything to the Sportswear Collection catalog, show your usual blanks
   as an in-house mini catalog, and still link to the full catalog.
7. Which blanks do you refuse to print on, or warn people off?
8. Are there garments beyond tees you print regularly? Hoodies, totes,
   bandanas, hats, anything else?

## Runs and one-offs

    Note: the product page should not lead with setup fees. Show the unit
    price and the breaks, fold setup into the proof. Sizes: offer 4XL where
    the blank comes in it, with the usual upcharge above 2XL, and let the
    shop set that upcharge in Pulse.

9. What is your true minimum for a run? Is there a number below which it is
   just not worth it?
10. Do you save screens and frames for reprints? For how long?
11. Do you want to offer one-off prints or small runs from designs you
    already have screens for? That is the Available prints idea.
12. Standard turnaround, rush turnaround, and what rush costs.
13. Colors per press, max print size, and the placements you offer.
14. What do people ask for that you do not do?

## Art and design

15. What do you charge for graphic design, and is it separate from the
    print job?
16. What do you charge for color separations only?
17. Do you work from anyone's finished design, or do you need to touch every
    file?
18. Do you want people uploading art through the site, emailing it, or both?

## Money and process

    Note: the flow is built as request, then run, then quote, then accept.
    The shop opens a run from a request, adds the garments and the numbers,
    hits Send the quote. The customer gets an email and a page at
    /proof/<token>/ with the job ticket and one Accept button. Accepting
    moves the run to awaiting payment and emails the shop. Ask Jack whether
    that matches how he actually quotes today, and whether he wants the
    quote to carry a deposit line.

19. Online payment, a quote and invoice after proof approval, or just be
    contacted and invoice after the proof? How does it ideally work for you?
20. Deposits on big runs?
21. Tax rate you charge, and do you ship or is it pickup only?
22. Do you take cards today, and with what? Square reader, a bank terminal,
    checks and cash? Nothing is wired on the site yet. For the buy it now
    items (the rack, the randoms) our lean is Stripe if you have no card
    system, Square if you already run a Square reader at the counter, so
    the shop and the site share one ledger.
23. Pricing. Every price on the site is a placeholder and every one of them
    is editable in Pulse: the base price per garment, the quantity breaks
    (24, 48, 72, 144 today), screen setup and the per location charge. What
    are your real numbers? Do you price by color count, by location, by
    garment, or a mix?

## Blanks, on hand and to order

24. What is on the shelf right now, by style, color and size? That becomes
    the on hand stock in Pulse, counted in dozens or pieces.
25. What do you usually order in, and from whom? Those styles get featured
    as the in house catalog, with the full Sportswear Collection a link
    away. We keep every color the catalog lists for each style.

## Photos and the feed

    Note: the photos we have are 365px web copies. The lightbox and any
    bigger tile need originals. Stopgap until then: screenshot the posts on
    Instagram at desktop size, which gives about 1000px, good enough for
    the wall, not for print. Ask for originals regardless.

26. Can we get the originals of the shirts on your Instagram feed, or access
    to the account, to pull them at full size?
27. Can you share a folder of photos you would like on the site and are
    happy to see used off site for marketing?
28. Who should keep the wall updated, and would you upload photos inside
    Pulse yourself?

## People and email

29. Who usually answers the phone?
30. Where should form submissions and order emails go? sales@fishbonegraphics.com?
31. Any additional emails that should get leads and form submissions?
32. Who needs a Pulse login, and what should each person be allowed to do?

## Calendar

33. Do you keep a calendar of festivals and events you print for? Would you
    want that on the site so people come to Fishbone to see what is in town?
34. Would a shop calendar in Pulse help, with blanks arriving, orders due,
    and jobs on press?
